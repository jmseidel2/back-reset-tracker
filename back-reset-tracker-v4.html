How does this work?

What exercises today?
The app automatically picks cervical/thoracic or lumbar based on the day of the week. You see it right at the top in the dark card.
When do I move to the next week?
When you feel the same or better than at the start of the week. If pain increases, stay on the current week.
Where's the original doctor's sheet?
Top right: "Sheet" button. Opens the original in fullscreen.
How do I see the correct form?
Tap an exercise → "GIF" button → opens Google image search for animated demonstrations.
Can't do it every day?
No problem. Streak resets, plan continues. Consistency over 30 days > perfection.
